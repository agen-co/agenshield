## What you see

One or more of these, for days at a time:

- The Mac's version in the Frontegg Portal does not advance after a new release.
- An update sent to the Mac from the Frontegg Portal shows as **timed out** or
  **failed**, and the Mac is still on its previous version.
- The AgenShield menu bar keeps saying an update is available, but nothing
  installs it.

The Mac is still protected throughout. A failed or never-started update leaves
the installed version running and enforcing — nothing is half-installed.

There are two different causes. Work out which one applies before you act.

## Cause 1 — no update was ever sent to the Mac

From version `2026.9.1`, AgenShield **never installs an update on its own**.
The Mac checks for new releases and tells you one exists, but it installs it
only when your organization asks it to:

- **Automatic updates** are turned on for your organization in the Frontegg
  Portal — they are **off by default**; or
- an administrator sends an update to the device, or starts an update for a
  group of devices, from the Frontegg Portal.

On `2026.9.0`, automatic installation was a per-device setting, also off by
default.

So a Mac that reports "update available" for a week is usually doing exactly
what it was configured to do. Nothing on the Mac is broken.

**How to confirm:** in the Frontegg Portal, check whether automatic updates are
enabled for your organization, and whether any update was sent to this device.
If neither is the case, this is your cause.

**How to fix:** turn on automatic updates, or send the update to the device from
the Frontegg Portal. Devices pick it up within a few minutes while they are
awake and online.

## Cause 2 — the update was interrupted while the Mac was asleep

A closed or idle Mac wakes briefly every few minutes for maintenance (to sync
mail, back up, and so on) and goes back to sleep after well under a minute.
An update sent from the Frontegg Portal can reach the Mac during one of those
brief wakes. On affected versions the download was then timed against the clock
**including the time the Mac spent asleep**, so it gave up as soon as the Mac
woke again, and the update was reported as failed (or never reported at all).

You may also see a message mentioning **GitHub Releases** and a `404` next to
the real error. For pre-release builds that part is expected and is not the
cause — those builds are published only to AgenShield's own download service.

**Affected versions:** `2026.10.2` and earlier.
**Fixed in:** the release after `2026.10.2`. From then on the download pauses
while the Mac sleeps and resumes when it wakes, an interrupted update is retried
automatically for up to about a day before it is reported as failed, and a
failure message names the download problem that actually occurred.

The fix applies to updates **from** a fixed version onward: a Mac still running
an affected version needs one more update sent while it is awake.

**How to confirm:** on the Mac, look at the end of the update log:

```bash
tail -n 5 /var/log/agenshield/upgrade.log
```

If the last lines say the download failed with `curl timed out after
300000ms`, followed by a report that the upgrade was aborted and the Mac is
still on its previous version, this is your cause.

**How to fix:** send the update again from the Frontegg Portal while the Mac is
**awake and in use** (or plugged in with the lid open), and leave it awake for a
few minutes. Alternatively, run the update directly on the Mac:

```bash
agenshield upgrade
```

## When to escalate

Contact support with a diagnostic bundle (see
[Collecting diagnostics](../troubleshoot/collecting-diagnostics.mdx)) if:

- an update sent to an **awake** Mac still fails, or keeps failing on a version
  later than `2026.10.2`;
- the update log shows a checksum or signature failure — never work around
  those; or
- the Mac never shows that a newer release exists even though one has been
  published for your organization's release channel.
